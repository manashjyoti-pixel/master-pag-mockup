# Workflow review verification

This is a standalone browser demonstration. Passing these checks does not verify production APIs, databases, provider accounts or delivery capacity.

## Completed

1. All 33 primary role/navigation combinations were populated and opened without browser page errors.
2. For each of Super Admin, managed service, white-label and SaaS: private and public proposal intake, research simulation, review, actual PDF generation, single email simulation, human reply, opportunity qualification, agreement simulation, service handoff, programme execution and refresh persistence passed browser checks.
3. Eight downloadable PDF examples were generated through the application. Each contains two pages; text-boundary inspection found no text outside the page safety margins. Representative private/public and white-label pages were rendered and visually inspected.
4. Scheduling from Outreach returns to history. A matched human reply pauses a pending scheduled message.
5. Chat-assisted intake saves the same proposal brief. Quoted CSV input imports private workspace companies; a saved segment carries its selected company IDs into programme setup.
6. A programme configured for 10,000 synthetic companies and 10,000 sends/day executed that demo day and created a representative actual PDF version. The remaining company volumes are virtual cohorts, not thousands of generated artifacts.
7. Evidence exceptions consume no send or preparation charge. Releasing a held follow-up preserves its minimum due date. Replacing one schedule leaves other companies unchanged.
8. A programme reply links the exact proposal version into the opportunity pipeline. Resource attachment requires approval of its illustrative quote; the calculated mailbox shortfall was verified.
9. Service catalogue approval works. PDF regeneration creates v2 while preserving the earlier approved snapshot. Workspace private records stay separate in browser state; Super Admin selects an acting workspace explicitly.
10. Mobile sidebar navigation works without horizontal page overflow.
11. ESLint and 16 domain tests passed. The production build passed. Dependency audit reported zero known vulnerabilities at validation time.

## Domain checks

Capacity and duplicate-day prevention; 14-day cadence from actual send; five-touch cap; hard end date; per-company replies; manual occurrence replacement; rescheduling without touch consumption; preparation holds; spending and total-email limits; immutable approved snapshots; required selected simulation; programme-rule approval; held-follow-up release; reply suppression; optional simulation cost.

## Intentionally simulated or deferred

- Discovery, website/search/SEO enrichment and AI writing use clearly labelled fixtures.
- MiroFish runs, graphs and findings are illustrative. Real integration remains part of the production roadmap.
- Credit charges, provider purchases, SMTP handoffs, delivery confirmations, receiving inboxes, replies, bookings, signatures and client/project handoffs are browser simulations.
- Browser storage is not production artifact storage or authentication. Role switching is for review, not access security.
- Translation options, automated WhatsApp/SMS, checkout and revenue-to-credit conversion are not enabled.
- Production must map the proposed adapters to verified current Engine, Scanner, email and agreement contracts. A four-month planning horizon requires additive validation support.
